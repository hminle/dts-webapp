Fiscal Briefing

A weekly snapshot, 14 more categories, rolling averages and the Treasury’s cash balance. These are the figures a weekly fiscal newsletter leads with, built from the same Treasury statements the app already serves.

The plain explanation

What exists today

The Treasury renamed and reorganized many lines between 2019 and 2023. Today an old year can be compared with zero, and nothing on screen says so.

Two new pages, one new daily view and new card grids. Thursday or Friday wherever a week is read.

The order of work

1 Fair comparisons→2 Categories+3 Snapshot
then4 Leading flowsand5 Cash balance

2 and 3 can be built at the same time, and so can 4 and 5.

What could go wrong

Treasury renames a line again→add the new name; the card says “comparable from”
Cash balance moves rows→the app stops with an error, never shows 0

Each risk has a loud answer. A missing number stays blank. It is never silently filled with zero.

Part 3

The developer briefing

Goal

Close the gaps between the app and the MMT Trader weekly briefing. Its method is reverse-engineered in docs/research/mmt-trader-dts-method.md. The plan adds 14 preset categories, a weekly snapshot, rolling averages, two combined figures and the Treasury General Account (TGA) balance. It first fixes category coverage across Treasury line renames. GDP comparisons are out of scope.

How the snapshot picks its dates

latest record_date ≤ as_of→anchor = latest Thu/Fri on or before it→statement in anchor−5 … anchor→none: 404
S→FY−1, same day_of_fiscal_year→missing: previous fields null
anchor − 7 → W→W in S’s fiscal year?yes →gap change = S gap − W gapno →null + reason

Coverage: the decision the rest stands on

A rename whose FYTD carries forward is merged: the old name goes into components. A restructure sets available_from, and totals before it are null. Breakdown rows stay raw, and the UI hides rows that are 0 in both years.

Key decisions

  • Null, not zero. A total before available_from, or with no aligned date, is null. The card says “Comparable from …”.
  • “Other”, not “major”. New categories get their own endpoints and grid. Each card carries its own formula id instead of a kind-to-formula map.
  • Thursday by default on the snapshot, matching the newsletter. Friday stays the default on the weekly comparison, so nothing existing changes.
  • The gap change stays inside one fiscal year. Across October 1 it is null with a reason.
  • Statements, not days. The 10-day average is the last 10 statements. The monthly rate is × 20, labelled as a rule of thumb.
  • We do not copy the newsletter’s errors. Its HUD and SNAP changes use one line where the total uses two. Ours use the same lines for both: SNAP & WIC on Dec 4, 2025 is −1,965, not −1,870.

Risks

Another Treasury rename

The new name goes first in components, plus a row in Phase 0’s history table. Until then the card reads “Comparable from”, which is wrong but visibly so.

The cash balance moves rows again

balance_on raises when a matched row has a NULL value, so a new era fails loudly in tests and Sentry.

Parallel phases touch categories.py

Phase 2 refactors the major endpoints and Phase 3 extracts _source_for. If both run at once, merge Phase 2 first.

Small differences from the newsletter

Our unemployment average for Dec 4, 2025 is 173.6; it printed 173.5. Ours is the documented definition, and the research note records the difference.
